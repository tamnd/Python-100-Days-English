## Problems and Solutions in Team Project Development

Team projects introduce challenges that do not appear in solo coding.

Common problems:

1. unclear requirements
2. inconsistent coding style
3. overlapping ownership
4. weak communication
5. unstable environments
6. uncontrolled merges and releases

Practical solutions:

- define responsibilities clearly
- maintain a specification or product document
- use version control properly
- review code
- automate tests and deployment where possible
- standardize environments and dependencies

A healthy team process matters as much as individual coding ability.
