## pandas from the Ground Up, Part 4

### Aggregation and Grouping

Grouping is central to business analysis.

Examples:

```python
df.groupby('department')['salary'].mean()
df.groupby(['city', 'gender']).agg({'age': 'mean', 'income': 'sum'})
```

Useful operations:

- `groupby`
- `agg`
- `count`
- `sum`
- `mean`
- `max`
- `min`

### Sorting and Ranking

```python
df.sort_values('score', ascending=False)
df['rank'] = df['score'].rank(ascending=False)
```

These tools support KPI summaries, segmentation, and many common reporting tasks.
