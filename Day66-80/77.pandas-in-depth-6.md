## pandas from the Ground Up, Part 6

### Time Series and Advanced Operations

pandas is especially strong for time-series work.

Typical operations:

- parsing dates
- setting datetime indices
- resampling
- rolling windows
- shifting and lagging

Examples:

```python
df['date'] = pd.to_datetime(df['date'])
df = df.set_index('date')
df.resample('M').sum()
df['rolling_avg'] = df['value'].rolling(7).mean()
```

Time-series features are essential for trend analysis, business monitoring, and forecasting preparation.
