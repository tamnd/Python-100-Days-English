## Data Visualization, Part 3

The final visualization lesson focuses on more practical charting patterns such as:

- multi-chart layouts
- statistical plots
- combining pandas and matplotlib
- exporting charts for reports

Typical workflow:

1. prepare clean data
2. choose an appropriate chart type
3. add labels and visual hierarchy
4. export or embed the result in reports

Visualization is the final delivery layer for much of descriptive data analysis.
