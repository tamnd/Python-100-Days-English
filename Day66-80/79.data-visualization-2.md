## Data Visualization, Part 2

This section continues with chart customization:

- titles
- labels
- line styles
- colors
- subplots
- figure size

Examples:

```python
plt.figure(figsize=(8, 4))
plt.bar(['A', 'B', 'C'], [5, 7, 3])
plt.xlabel('Category')
plt.ylabel('Value')
plt.title('Bar Chart')
plt.show()
```

The goal is to make plots more readable and better suited for reports and presentations.
