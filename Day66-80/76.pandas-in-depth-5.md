## pandas from the Ground Up, Part 5

### Merging and Joining

pandas supports SQL-like table combination:

- `merge`
- `join`
- `concat`

Examples:

```python
pd.merge(df1, df2, on='id')
pd.concat([df1, df2], axis=0)
```

### Reshaping

Useful reshaping tools:

- `pivot`
- `pivot_table`
- `melt`
- `stack`
- `unstack`

These operations are especially useful for turning raw tables into report-ready layouts.
