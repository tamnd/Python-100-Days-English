## NumPy in Practice, Part 2

### Descriptive Statistics

NumPy provides convenient methods for common descriptive statistics.

Example:

```python
array1 = np.random.randint(1, 100, 10)
```

Common statistics:

- sum: `array1.sum()` or `np.sum(array1)`
- mean: `array1.mean()` or `np.mean(array1)`
- median: `np.median(array1)`
- quantile: `np.quantile(array1, 0.5)`

Extremes and spread:

- max: `array1.max()`
- min: `array1.min()`
- range: `np.ptp(array1)`
- interquartile range: compute `q3 - q1`

Variance and standard deviation:

- `array1.var()`
- `array1.std()`

### Frequency and Distribution

Other useful tools include:

- sorting
- counting
- histograms
- binning

These functions help summarize the central tendency, spread, and distribution of numerical data before more advanced modeling.
