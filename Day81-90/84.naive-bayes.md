## Naive Bayes

Naive Bayes is a probabilistic classifier based on Bayes’ theorem and a strong independence assumption between features.

Why it is useful:

- simple
- fast
- often surprisingly effective for text classification

Typical variants:

- Gaussian Naive Bayes
- Multinomial Naive Bayes
- Bernoulli Naive Bayes

Common use cases:

- spam filtering
- document classification
- basic sentiment tasks

Its weakness is the unrealistic assumption that features are independent, but in many practical problems it still works well enough to be useful.
