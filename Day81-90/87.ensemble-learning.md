## Ensemble Learning Algorithms

Ensemble learning combines multiple weak or base models to build a stronger predictor.

Common ensemble strategies:

1. bagging
2. boosting
3. stacking

Examples:

- Random Forest
- AdaBoost
- Gradient Boosting
- XGBoost-style methods

Why ensembles help:

- reduce variance
- reduce bias
- improve generalization

The tradeoff is that ensembles are usually less interpretable than a single simple model.
