## The k-Nearest Neighbors Algorithm

KNN is a simple supervised learning algorithm used for classification and regression.

Basic idea:

1. choose `k`
2. measure distance between the new sample and training samples
3. find the `k` nearest points
4. classify by majority vote or predict by local averaging

Common distance metrics:

- Euclidean distance
- Manhattan distance

Advantages:

- easy to understand
- no explicit training phase

Disadvantages:

- slow on large data sets
- sensitive to feature scaling
- can perform poorly in high-dimensional spaces

KNN is often a good teaching algorithm because it makes the intuition of local similarity very clear.
