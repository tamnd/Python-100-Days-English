## Introduction to Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing (NLP) focuses on how computers process human language.

Typical tasks:

- tokenization
- part-of-speech tagging
- text classification
- sentiment analysis
- named-entity recognition
- machine translation

Classical NLP often uses:

- word frequencies
- TF-IDF
- bag-of-words models

Modern NLP often uses:

- embeddings
- transformers
- large pretrained language models

NLP combines linguistics, machine learning, and practical engineering for text-heavy problems.
