## Neural Network Models

Neural networks are layered function approximators inspired loosely by biological neurons.

Basic components:

- input layer
- hidden layers
- output layer
- weights
- bias
- activation functions

Training usually involves:

- forward propagation
- loss calculation
- backpropagation
- gradient-based optimization

Neural networks are powerful for:

- images
- language
- speech
- nonlinear prediction tasks

But they also require:

- more data
- more compute
- more tuning

Compared with classical models, they are less interpretable but often more powerful on complex tasks.
